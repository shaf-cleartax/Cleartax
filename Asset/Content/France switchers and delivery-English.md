# **01  Before you go live**

For every other vendor, e-invoicing is one product in a catalogue. For Cleartax it is the whole company. That distinction matters most before go-live, because choosing an inefficient PA leaves an enterprise exposed to three gaps that compound quietly until the deadline arrives.

| 01  Skill Gap ERP and tax expertise rarely live in one team. The people who know your ERP inside out are seldom the people who know French VAT and DGFiP rules, so the platform is left to bridge a gap it was never built to close. |
| :---- |

| 02  Delayed go-lives ERP readiness and data dependencies stall timelines. When the platform cannot absorb your source-system complexity, integration slips, sign-offs drag, and a fixed regulatory date turns into a scramble. |
| :---- |

| 03  Data complexity Master data, IDs and references break across scattered systems. Wrong or missing VAT IDs, branches on the wrong country code, inactive or duplicated Peppol IDs, addresses pointing to the wrong PA — the supplier directory is wrong before a single invoice is sent. |
| :---- |

| These three gaps do not stay separate. Left in an inefficient PA, they compound into business continuity risk: delayed invoice clearance, AR/AP slowdowns, and mounting penalty exposure. In France that means around €15 per missed e-invoice and €250 to €500 per missed e-reporting transmission, plus input-VAT credit denials once the mandate bites. The relief of making the deadline fades fast; the same patterns then repeat, in every cycle, every time. |
