**Overview**

This is the story of one of India’s largest e-commerce marketplaces. It connects lakhs of sellers with buyers across the country, and that reach puts real weight on its finance team. The scale is enormous. Every quarter the business processes around 4.6 lakh tax deductions across more than 3.2 lakh vendors, and it adjusts close to 3 lakh debit notes a month, worth about ₹3 crore.

At this size, tax deducted at source is far more than paperwork. Under the TDS mandate, the company must deduct tax at the correct rate on vendor payments, apply valid Lower Deduction Certificates where they exist, deposit the tax through challans, and file accurate quarterly returns. Get a rate or a certificate wrong at this volume, and the tax department responds with demand notices. As the business grew, that pressure kept building, quarter after quarter.

**What was the requirement**

Every quarter the same problems returned, and most came down to volume meeting manual work. Tax was often deducted at a lower rate, but the correct LDC details were not always carried into the filing. That single gap was enough to trigger demand notices worth close to ₹1.45 crore in some quarters.

The manual work ran deeper still. Each debit note had to be mapped at section and rate level by hand, which took six to seven person-days every quarter. Because the process was manual, three to four corrections had to be filed each quarter, and a full person was set aside just to manage them. Invalid PANs, missing LDC validation and over-used challans added more risk, and frequent team changes meant hard-won mapping knowledge often left with the person who held it.

The company needed a way to handle this scale with ease, remove the manual grind, and file accurately every quarter without the last-minute fixes.

**How did ClearTax help**

ClearTax stepped in with a fix built around how this team actually filed, not an off-the-shelf template. At the centre was a custom Excel and VBA tool that automated debit note mapping at PAN, section and rate level, removing the manual step that had caused most of the errors.

Alongside it, PAN status and LDC validation ran through ClearTax’s API, so deduction rates were checked and corrected before any return was filed. One-click challan import with manual tagging stopped challans from being over-consumed, and standardised data formats with pre-filing completeness checks removed the back-and-forth that used to slow every filing. A vendor hygiene process kept the master data clean for the long term.

All of this sits on top of what ClearTax already does at enterprise scale. The platform syncs LDC and challan consumption, verifies PANs in bulk, and runs more than two hundred checks before a return is generated, including checks that flag potential short deductions.
