**THE SHIFT**

**On 1 April 2026, the audit changed its question.**

For sixty-five years the tax audit asked a qualitative question. Did you deduct? Did you file? A tick in a box, anchored to materiality and professional judgement. From Tax Year 2026-27 that framework is gone. The Income-tax Act, 2025 has replaced the 1961 Act, and **Form 26 has replaced Forms 3CA, 3CB and 3CD entirely**.

Under the old Clause 34(b), the auditor confirmed whether Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) returns were filed. Yes or no. Under **Clause 50(b) of Form 26**, the auditor must now report the total number of transactions in the TDS/TCS statements after the latest correction, **the exact count of transactions not reported, and the monetary value attached to each of them**.

| *Quantification has replaced qualification. Compliance has moved from declaration to demonstration.* |
| :---- |

**What actually changed under your feet**

| Compliance artefact | Until 31 Mar 2026 | From 1 Apr 2026 |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Governing statute | Income-tax Act, 1961 (819 sections) | **Income-tax Act, 2025 (536 sections)** |
| TDS section architecture | 192–196D and the entire 194 series | **Sections 392, 393, 394, table-driven** |
| Transaction referencing | Section numbers (194C, 194J, 194I…) | **Numeric payment codes 1001–1067** |
| Quarterly returns | Form 24Q, 26Q, 27Q, 27EQ | **Form 138, Form 140 and the renumbered series** |
| Tax audit report | Form 3CA / 3CB / 3CD (44 clauses) | **Form 26 under Rule 47, UDIN mandatory** |
| TDS/TCS disclosure | Clause 34, Narrative format with Yes/No | **Clauses 49, 50, 51 with count and value** |
| GST-linked expenditure | Clause 44 of Form 3CD | **Schedule VI of Form 26** |
| Interest / disallowance | Sections 201(1A) and 40(a)(ia) | **Sections 398(3)(a) and 35(b)** |

**THE STANDARD**

**What "owning the audit" actually means**

Owning the audit is not about surviving it. It is about who supplies the answer.

Today the auditor supplies it, test-checking on materiality, backed by judicial reference and professional judgment, because your data cannot answer the question directly. That is how 90% of companies end up carrying a qualification.

Owning the audit means every question the auditor asks resolves to **a record you already hold,** not a reconstruction, not a reason.

**Six conditions, and what each demands of your systems**

| Condition | What it means | What it demands operationally |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Tax strategy established** | Documented, owned at board level | A stated risk appetite for deduction accuracy, and a metric reporting against it |
| **Applied comprehensively** | Every transaction affecting the tax position is governed | Coverage of 100% of postings; not sampled, not materiality-based |
| **Responsibility assigned** | Board accountable for design, tax team for operation | Clear ownership across tax, AP, procurement and controllership |
| **Governance documented** | Explicit, resourced, periodically reviewed | Version-controlled rule logic, not tribal knowledge in a lookup table |
| **Testing performed** | Controls evidenced as operating | Continuous exception reporting with audit trails, not an annual review |
| **Assurance provided** | The output of the other five | A reproducible answer to any auditor question, on demand |

*Framework: OECD (2016), Co-operative Tax Compliance: Building Better Tax Control Frameworks. Operational interpretation and maturity model: ClearTax.*

**The four levels of audit ownership**

Most enterprises can place themselves on this ladder in under a minute. The level you sit at determines what you can produce when an assessment arrives.

| Level | How exceptions are found | What you can produce | Audit outcome |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **One: Reactive** | The department finds them first | An explanation | **Qualification** |
| **Two: Documented** | Manual review, sampled at year-end | A narrative note | **Negotiated** |
| **Three: Automated** | Post-facto reconciliation after filing | A reconstruction | Defensible with effort |
| **Four: In control** | Validation before payment releases | **A record** | **Nothing to defend** |

Level three is where most large Indian enterprises now sit, and it was sufficient under the 1961 Act. Under Clause 50(b) it is not. A reconstruction cannot produce an exact count of unreported transactions and their value across a full year of postings.

**Where validation sits decides what it actually is**

This is the whole argument, and it is a question of placement rather than capability. The same PAN check, the same threshold test, the same section logic produces a completely different outcome depending on where in the transaction lifecycle it runs.

| At filing → a reconciliation The check runs against a return that is already prepared. It tells you what is wrong in a document. The deduction happened weeks ago and the money has gone. After payment → a correction Exposure has already crystallised. Interest is already accruing from the date of default. Fixing it now needs vendor coordination, a debit note and a revised statement, inside a window that is now two years, not six. Between posting and payment → a control The only point at which the exception can still be resolved without consequence. No interest, no vendor conversation, no correction statement, nothing to disclose in Clause 50(b).  |
| :---- |

**The market has largely built the first two**

Compute TDS in the ERP without structured exception logic, then reconcile discrepancies after payment. Neither addresses the problem where it originates, at the transaction, before funds move.

| 01 | A single, raw pipe from the ledger *Data acquisition, not extraction* — One connector carrying raw GL data — not pre-filtered TDS, expense and GST extracts from separate extractors. — Multi-ERP, OMS and POS coverage, so SAP, Oracle and Dynamics entities resolve to one schema. — A trial balance match proving 100% of the source was captured, before any downstream logic is trusted. |
| :---- | :---- |

| 02 | Tax-critical tagging at the point of posting *Prevent* — Every expense auto-tagged with the attributes that decide tax treatment: advance, prepaid, provision, reversal, net-off, capital versus P\&L. — GRN-to-IR linkage resolved, including one-to-many and many-to-many cases, so expense and purchase accounting can be stitched. — Tagging happens at posting — a transaction that reaches filing untagged cannot be classified without human archaeology. |
| :---- | :---- |

| 03 | Statutory logic that is versioned, not remembered *Rule governance* — Payment-code mapping against the Income-tax Act, 2025 (codes 1001 to 1067 under Sections 392, 393, 394\) with dated effectivity so pre- and post-April-2026 transactions resolve correctly. — AI-assisted section variance detection for the borderline cases a lookup table cannot catch: technical services versus consulting, manpower supply versus works contract. — Threshold logic evaluated cumulatively across entities and periods, not per transaction. |
| :---- | :---- |

| 04 | Real-time validation before funds move *The control itself* — PAN status, PAN-Aadhaar operability and LDC limit checked live at the moment of deduction, not at filing. — Automated rate determination combining section, PAN status, threshold position, LDC availability and statutory logic. — Exception flagged between expense posting and payment release, the only window in which correction carries no consequence. |
| :---- | :---- |

| 05 | Continuous reconciliation, not annual reconstruction *Detect* — Expense GL against purchase register and GSTR-2B monthly, keeping Schedule VI defensible without a year-end scramble. — 26AS against TDS receivable with many-to-many matching, best-fit and cross-year logic, and automated PAN-to-TAN mapping. — Short deduction, non-deduction and section inconsistency alerts generated as they occur. |
| :---- | :---- |

| 06 | A transaction-level ledger of what was reported *Clause 50(b) readiness* — Every original and correction return mapped back to the individual GL entry that produced it. — Transactions reported, transactions not reported and the value of each, maintained as a live system output, not compiled in March. — Corrections flagged so nothing falls between an original filing and its revision, inside the shortened two-year window. |
| :---- | :---- |

| 07 | Immutable snapshots and an evidence trail *Assurance* — Point-in-time snapshots of every filing, one source of truth for audit queries, notice replies and diligence. — Full lineage from statutory disclosure back to the originating ledger entry, so every auditor question resolves to a record, not an explanation. — Reporting the board can read: exceptions prevented, defaults outstanding, notices open, credits recovered. |
