# **Overview**

This is the story of Zomato, one of India's largest food technology companies. It runs a leading online platform that supplies restaurants across the country. The scale is enormous. The business handles more than 1.2 crore invoices every year, works with over 1000 vendors, and files across more than 30 GST registrations.

At this size, tax compliance is far more than paperwork. It shapes cash flow, working capital, and the risk the finance team carries each month. For years the company leaned on one of the Big 4 firms to run its compliance and the technology behind it. As the business grew, that arrangement began to strain.

# **What was the requirement**

The finance team pulled sales data from the Order Management System and purchase data from SAP. Bringing the two together was slow and heavily manual. Before anything could reach the Big 4 team, someone had to shape the data in Excel by hand. At this volume, that work stretched the team thin.

The tools in use could not keep up with the size of the data. Large files brought errors, and filings kept slipping to the last minute. The company needed a way to bring both systems together, handle the scale with ease, and file on time every month without the manual grind.

# **The consequences it had**

The old setup created problems that stacked up month after month.

| Manual data prep Shaping large data by hand in Excel drained the team's time and let errors slip in. | Last-minute filings The sheer size of the data slowed things down, so filings kept running to the wire. |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Repeat amendments** Manual fixes rolled into next-month amendments to GSTR-1 and raised the risk of scrutiny. | **Blocked ITC** Unadjusted transactions kept blocking input tax credit and locked up working capital. |

# **How did ClearTax help**

ClearTax stepped in with GL-Stream, an integration layer built for enterprise compliance. It connects multiple source systems into a single flow. It then aligns the data with the general ledger, so compliance runs on one clean, consistent set of records.

Once the systems were connected, the company could reconcile its sales and purchase records against the general ledger, even where those records first came from the Order Management System. From there, the platform prepared GSTR-1 and Table 4 of GSTR-3B automatically, based on the actions the team took and built on data that GL-Stream had already transformed and made ready.
