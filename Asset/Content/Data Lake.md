THE REGULATORY CLOCK
Jul 2025
GSTR-3B liability hard-locked. Outward-
liability tables became non-editable;
corrections now happen upstream via
GSTR-1A, before filing.
Oct 2025
The law moved too. Section 38 amended:
your ITC is now legally tied to the records
you accept (or are deemed to accept) in
IMS.
Apr 2026
IMS became mandatory. ITC is allowed
strictly per GSTR-2B; the portal hard-
blocks mismatched claims. Inaction counts
as acceptance — a data error costs cash,
monthly.
2026 (expected)
ITC hard-locking. GSTR-3B’s ITC table is
slated to lock to GSTR-2B. Mismatches
stop being fixable inside the return — at
all.
The era of “fix it in the return” is over. The
new rule is verify before you file, or file
wrong, permanently.
