**Why this gap matters**

**Silent non-compliance at scale.** With 70% lacking any automated response loop, the maths compounds fast. In a mid-size enterprise handling 5,000 monthly invoices, 250 monthly rejections at an average AED 50,000 value with a 50% resolution delay lock roughly AED 6.25M per month in unresolved receivables, each one a transaction that, in the FTA's records, never occurred.

**Late ERP discovery doubles the timeline.** If the ERP gap is found before vendor selection it costs a one to two week RFP delay. Found after selection it means an 8 to 16 week implementation delay and a 2 to 3 times cost overrun. With only months to the ASP deadline, an integration planned for 8 weeks that actually needs 16 compresses the remaining programme to breaking point.

**Archiving becomes an audit emergency.** An FTA request for specific invoices in machine-readable format within 48 hours currently means 20 to 40 hours reconstructing data from PDF archives and transaction logs. Missing the window triggers a violation of archiving requirements.

**How to move from awareness to action**

**Start now: conduct a structured ERP gap analysis (the gating item)**

Before issuing any vendor RFP, answer with your IT and ERP teams: can the ERP generate PINT AE-compliant XML today, tested on both simple and complex invoices; what is the internal and vendor resourcing; where are the data-mapping gaps; and what is the path forward. The paths are a native upgrade (6 to 12 weeks), a middleware or ASP layer (8 to 16 weeks), or system replacement (16 to 32 weeks plus data migration). The analysis itself takes 2 to 4 weeks and lets you select a vendor matched to your actual ERP situation and budget correctly.

**Month 2 to 3: build the response-handling loop**

Integrate with the FTA response channel, automate parsing of clearance, rejection and conditional-clearance responses, route rejections to corrective workflows by reason (format error, missing data, tax-calculation error), and track resubmissions to closure. Around 4 to 6 weeks, and it eliminates the silent-rejection exposure.

**Month 3 to 4: design audit-ready archiving**

Migrate from PDF archives to structured machine-readable storage, implement jurisdiction-specific retention and retrieval protocols, build automated extraction for FTA requests, and test a 48-hour retrieval SLA with sample requests. Around 6 to 8 weeks for design, migration and testing.
