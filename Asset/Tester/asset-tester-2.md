## Slide 3 --- SmartFile Builds Your Tax Profile Automatically

### Headline

**Upload once. SmartFile connects the financial dots.**

### Supporting text

SmartFile reads available tax documents, categorises financial
information, and creates a unified tax profile for review.

### Process

**1. Import**\
Upload Form 16 and financial documents or connect available tax data.

**2. Understand**\
AI identifies salary, interest, capital gains, investments, and
deductions.

**3. Validate**\
Smart checks highlight missing information and possible mismatches.

**4. Prepare**\
A structured tax return is generated and made ready for taxpayer review.

### Visual instructions

Design a horizontal four-step data flow. Start with document thumbnails
on the left. Show glowing data particles or structured lines moving
through an AI processing layer in the centre. End with a clean tax
profile dashboard on the right. Use small labels for Salary, Capital
Gains, Interest, and Deductions as data gets categorised.

### Suggested image

Abstract AI data-processing visual showing financial documents being
scanned, interpreted, and converted into clean structured tax
categories.
