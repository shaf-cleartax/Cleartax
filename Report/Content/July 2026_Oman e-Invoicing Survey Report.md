**PHASE 1  |  IMMEDIATE ACTIONS**

**What to Do in the First 30 Days**

For Phase 1 companies managing live gaps, the sequence matters. Solving the wrong problem first extends the exposure window.

**01  Get an ASP contracted and integrated.**

This is the single most urgent action for any Phase 1 company not yet transmitting through an OTA-accredited provider. As of July 2026, Oman has 11 accredited ASPs. Every day without one is a day of legally invalid invoicing.

**02  Triage the rejection queue.**

Pull the OTA rejection log and classify by type: format errors, missing mandatory fields, credit note linkage failures, and FX or intercompany gaps. The classification tells you where ERP configuration is failing. Every open rejection is a void invoice and an uncollectible receivable until resolved.

**03  Assign post-go-live ownership.**

Assign a named individual or team to own the OTA response loop. Set a rejection resolution SLA. Build a weekly reconciliation cadence between OTA clearance data and your ERP. This function needs a permanent owner, not a shared inbox.

**04  Run a data reconciliation check now.**

Cross-reference your e-invoice data against your last VAT return before the OTA does it for you. In KSA, one tax head at a major retail enterprise spent a full month reconstructing records after ZATCA requested an explanation of gaps. Do not let the authority find the discrepancy first.

**PRECAUTIONARY GUIDE  |  WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE**

**Five Features of a Finance Function**  
**That Is Genuinely Ready**

Going live on time is the minimum bar. These five traits separate organisations that manage Fawtara in production from those that are managed by it.

**01  End-to-End Visibility**

Every invoice is traceable from source transaction through ERP, through PEPPOL, through OTA submission, and back to the accounting entry. No dark segments. No assumptions about what happened between systems.

**02  Continuous Monitoring**

Exceptions are flagged in real time, not at period close. Anomalies surface automatically, investigated systematically, and resolved with a documented audit trail. The compliance team is not waiting to be surprised.

**03  Data-Level Validation**

Tax logic is validated against live transactions, not assumed correct because it was configured at go-live. In KSA, mapping errors silently propagated across thousands of invoices before discovery. Configuration is not a one-time activity.

**04  Defensible Audit Trails**

Every invoice is traceable, explainable, and evidenced quickly. If the OTA requested a full transaction-level reconciliation of your e-invoices against your VAT return for the last quarter, how long would it take? If the answer is days or weeks, that is the gap Phase 2 will find.

**05  Scalable Governance**

Controls work consistently across invoice volumes, legal entities, and geographies. The architecture adapts as Fawtara evolves without a full rebuild. This is the distinction between a platform built for e-invoicing and a connector bolted onto a general enterprise system.

**Start before the notification arrives.**

Every week of runway before go-live is a week of lower implementation cost. Phase 1 confirmed this. Organisations that started 12 or more weeks before their deadline outperformed those that started under 8 weeks on cost, rejection rates, and post-go-live stability.

**Run the ERP gap analysis before selecting any vendor.**

The gap analysis defines your integration path and your budget. It must precede vendor selection. Selecting a vendor before understanding your ERP's PINT-OM capability is the single most predictable source of mid-programme cost overrun.

**Map complex transaction types before configuration begins.**

Credit notes, self-billing, intercompany, FX-denominated invoices, B2G flows. Map each against PINT-OM requirements before configuration starts. Configuration built without this mapping will need rework when these transactions surface in testing or in live operations.

**Design the post-go-live operations function before go-live.**

Model your monthly rejection load. Build a reconciliation cadence. Assign ownership of the OTA response loop. This function needs to be designed in the implementation phase, not improvised after it.

**Build the full total cost of ownership before the board presentation.**

ASP licence, ERP configuration (typically 2 to 3 times the licence cost), internal resource time, testing, archiving migration, and post-go-live operations. The board presentation built on all five categories is the one that does not return six months later as an emergency top-up request.
