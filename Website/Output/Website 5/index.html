UAE e-invoicing mandate at a glance

How things are done, reimagined.

The UAE's shift to structured e-invoicing marks a fundamental change in how businesses generate, validate, and report invoices — building a finance function that's faster, more accurate, and ready for what comes next.

Model

Peppol-based 5-corner, decentralised continuous transaction control & exchange (DCTCE).

Scope & flow

B2B & B2G only. Supplier → supplier's ASP → buyer's ASP → buyer, with data routed to the FTA.

Format

Structured XML/UBL using the UAE PINT AE data dictionary — no more PDF invoices.

Compliance roadmap

From pilot to full enforcement

Jul 1, 2026

Pilot program start

A pilot begins for selected businesses. Others may voluntarily participate from this date.

Jul 31, 2026

Appoint ASP — large business

Deadline for businesses with revenue ≥ AED 50M to appoint an Accredited Service Provider.

Jan 1, 2027

Mandatory for large business

All businesses with annual revenue of AED 50M or more must fully comply.

Mar 31, 2027

Appoint ASP — smaller & govt

Deadline for smaller businesses and government entities to appoint an ASP.

Jul 1, 2027

Mandatory — smaller business

Businesses with annual revenue under AED 50M must comply.

Oct 1, 2027

Mandatory — government

Government entities must comply with the e-invoicing mandate.
