UAE e-invoicing mandate at a glance

Find how things are done in the UAE.

The UAE's e-invoicing mandate is structured, and invoicing marks a fundamental change in how businesses generate, validate, and report invoices. What seems like a compliance update is, in reality, a move toward real-time, digital-first tax reporting. Getting ready isn't just about meeting compliance — it's about building a finance function that's faster, more accurate, and ready for what comes next.

Model

Peppol-based 5-corner, also called a decentralized continuous transaction control and exchange model (DCTCE).

Scope & flow

Primarily B2B and B2G invoices — B2C is currently out of scope. Supplier → supplier's ASP → buyer's ASP → buyer, with relevant data sent to the FTA.

Format

Invoices must be in a structured format (XML/JSON) using the UAE PINT AE data dictionary — no more PDF invoices.

UAE 2027 guide

From pilot to full enforcement

Deadlines and milestones every business needs to track on the road to mandatory compliance.

Jul 1, 2026

Pilot program start

A pilot program begins for selected businesses. Other businesses can voluntarily participate from this date.

The pilot begins in July 2026, with phased enforcement by business size — entities earning above AED 50M are followed by smaller registered businesses in January and April 2027 respectively.

Jul 31, 2026

Appoint ASP — large business

Deadline for large businesses (revenue > AED 50 million) to appoint an Accredited Service Provider (ASP).

All VAT-registered businesses must appoint an ASP with the Federal Tax Authority before implementation. The ASP converts invoices to XML or JSON and sends them directly to the FTA, following Peppol standards like UBL and PINT.

Jan 1, 2027

Mandatory — large business

All businesses with an annual revenue of more than AED 50 million must comply.

ERP systems must produce structured invoice data in XML or JSON, map every field to the Ministry's data dictionary, apply a digital signature, and link each invoice for verification — unstructured invoices are no longer valid.

Mar 31, 2027

Appoint ASP — smaller & govt

Deadline for businesses with revenue < AED 50 million and government entities to appoint an ASP.

From July to December 2026, businesses should test system integration with the ASP and FTA sandbox — verifying data accuracy, error handling, and team readiness ahead of full rollout.

Jul 1, 2027

Mandatory — smaller business

Businesses with annual revenue up to AED 50 million must comply.

E-invoices and credit notes must be stored within the UAE, as required by the Tax Procedures Law — offshore hosting is prohibited. Implement secure archiving, access controls, and retrieval processes for audits.

Oct 1, 2027

Mandatory — government entities

Government entities must comply with the mandate.

Full Continuous Transaction Control enforcement across every entity type — pilot learnings, ASP integrations, and data governance built in the earlier phases carry through to steady-state compliance.
