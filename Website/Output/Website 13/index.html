01 — Logo

Logo

Our wordmark is the clearest signal of who we are. Keep it clean, keep it legible, and give it room to breathe.

Download logo files↓
On navy
On off-white

Clear space

Keep a minimum clear space equal to the height of the wordmark on all sides. Never let other elements crowd the logo.

Minimum size

Never scale the wordmark below 80px wide on screen, or 20mm wide in print — legibility comes first.

Do

  • Use the white mark on navy or dark photography
  • Use the navy mark on off-white or light backgrounds
  • Scale the logo proportionally

Don't

  • Stretch, skew, or rotate the wordmark
  • Recolour it outside navy or white
  • Place it on a busy or low-contrast background

02 — Color

We're navy & off-white with a dash of colour.

Generally speaking, our brand is navy and off-white. Simple and fundamental. Our accent colours are predominantly utilised to highlight information, draw attention to something, or provide an interruption. As a rule of thumb, if your layout starts looking like a rainbow, you've probably gone too far.

Violet is reserved exclusively for calls to action. Every button, link, and interactive prompt uses this colour so people always know where to click.

Colour ratios

Off-white 50% Navy 35% Violet 10% Lime 5%

Off-white covers the majority of any layout as empty space, navy carries the primary brand voice, violet appears on calls to action, and lime is the sparing accent that highlights key elements.

Colour combinations

These are the approved combinations. Don't put colour on colour — on a coloured background, always use navy or off-white text, whichever gives the greatest contrast.
