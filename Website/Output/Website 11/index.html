Compliance Roadmap

from pilot to full enforcement

The UAE's e-invoicing transition unfolds in phases. Each milestone marks a shift in requirements. Missing a deadline costs more than time.

Pilot program start

A pilot program begins for selected businesses. Other businesses can voluntarily participate from this date.

July 1, 2026

Appoint ASP for smaller businesses and government entities

Deadline for businesses with revenue < AED 50 million and government entities to appoint an ASP.

July 1, 2026

Mandatory e-invoicing for smaller businesses

Businesses with an annual revenue of less than AED 50 million must comply.

July 1, 2026

Mandatory e-invoicing for government entities

Government entities must comply with the mandate.

July 1, 2026

Appoint ASP for large businesses

Deadline for large businesses (revenue > AED 50 million) to appoint an Accredited Service Provider.

July 31, 2026

Mandatory e-invoicing for large businesses

All businesses with an annual revenue of more than AED 50 million must comply.

January 1, 2027
