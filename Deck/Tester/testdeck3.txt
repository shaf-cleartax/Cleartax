Slide 3: Regulation Is Reshaping Finance Operations

The UAE’s digital tax transformation is changing how finance teams think about invoicing, data and ERP architecture.

Before

Invoice created → Manually reviewed → Tax validated → Reported later

The emerging model

Transaction created → Validated digitally → Exchanged electronically → Data available in real time

Bottom insight:
Compliance can no longer sit at the end of the finance process. It needs to be embedded into the transaction flow.
