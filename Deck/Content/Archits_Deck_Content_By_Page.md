Country Key Milestones (indicative timelines\*) Saudi Arabia (KSA) • Dec
2020: Regulations introduced. • Dec 2021: Phase 1 (Generation) required
--- e-invoices & e-notes generation & record-keeping. • 1 Jan 2023:
Phase 2 begins --- Integration / transmission to ZATCA system.\
• 2023--2025: Phase 2 rolled out in waves (various revenue thresholds).
Recent waves:    -- Wave 18 (turnover \> SAR2m) compliance by 1 June
2025.    -- Wave 21 (turnover \> SAR1.25m) compliance by 30 November
2025. UAE • Oct / Nov 2024: Laws & portal launched; regulatory changes
introduced (Decree-Laws amendments). • 2025: Preparation (accreditation
of service providers, technical speciﬁcations) to occur. • July 2026:
Phase 1 go-live anticipated (mandatory e-invoicing begins for initial
taxpayer groups). • Beyond 2026: More phased inclusion, possibly
expanded scope (smaller taxpayers / B2C, etc.). Oman • Aug 2025: Tax
Authority working with ﬁrst \~100 large taxpayers for test phase. • Sep
2026: First mandatory wave for large taxpayers, B2B model based on
Peppol. • 2027--2028: Subsequent waves expanding to more B2B taxpayers,
B2G, etc. Other • Bahrain: Planning stage --- likely following Oman/UAE.
(No conﬁrmed dates yet.) • Qatar: Consultation in progress; timeline
likely aligned with VAT implementation phases. E-invoicing in Middle
