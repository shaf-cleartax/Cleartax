Problem Statement: 

Autonomous Orchestration of Month-End Statutory Tax Accounting under Multi-State GST Compliance

The monthly book-closure cycle for an indirect-tax function is a high-stakes, low-tolerance process demanding the reconciliation of filed GST returns (GSTR-3B, GSTR-6) against the general ledger, followed by the generation of statutorily compliant journal entries across multiple GL codes, cost centres, profit centres, and state-wise GSTINs. Each cycle requires the manual interpretation of return summaries, the correct netting of input tax credit against various GST liability accounts, the application of Rule 42 reversal logic for ineligible ITC, the distribution of ISD credits across recipient states, and the transformation of all of this into a 32-column ERP-ready SAP posting file — with zero margin for error, since every entry is subject to audit and statutory scrutiny.

Today this is executed manually by skilled tax professionals, consuming 1.5–2 days per month, and creating a single point of dependency, key-person risk, and a persistent exposure to transcription and classification errors.
